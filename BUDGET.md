# Slowth — Approved Budget v5

**Funding request: USD 10,000, one time.**  
**Delivery period: six months after receipt of funding.**

The main design and development work is planned for the first three months. The remaining three months are allocated to testing, independent build verification, refinements and fixes. Earlier completion is possible; the planned delivery window remains six months.

## Scope and expected results

### UX/UI Design — $2,100

60 hours × $35. UX research and review, clearer onboarding, explanations of blocking and pauses, navigation and settings, prototypes, user testing and implementation-ready screen designs. Expected result: a clearer setup and control experience that can be implemented within the development budget.

### iOS/macOS Development — $2,100

60 hours × $35. Implement the UX improvements, fix bugs and improve compatibility, including iPad. Expected result: working interface changes and reliability improvements in the existing Apple-platform app.

### Machine Learning — $1,750

50 hours × $35. Improve dataset quality, content recognition, model evaluation, accuracy and performance. Expected result: evaluated recognition improvements with documented results and limitations; no unmeasured accuracy target is promised.

### Promotion & Outreach — $1,250

Demonstration materials, publications, community outreach and small advertising experiments. Expected result: materials and outreach that help people discover and understand Slowth. Advertising is explicitly included and is not presented as development; no audience-growth guarantee is made.

### Android Development — $1,050

30 hours × $35. Research Android platform constraints and produce an experimental blocking prototype. Expected result: feasibility findings and a prototype, not a production Android release.

### Independent Build Verification — $1,000

An independent specialist reviews building from public source, dependencies, CI/CD and release procedures. Expected result: documented findings and recommended fixes. This is not a full security audit or a guarantee that an App Store binary is identical to a locally built binary.

### BrowserStack & Testing — $300

Up to $50/month for six months for cloud-device testing and testing tools, targeting iPhone, iPad and Android where supported. Remote-device support for ReplayKit/screen broadcast must be verified. Free open-source access will be explored before spending. Expected result: documented compatibility checks and issue reports. No physical-device purchases or separate external QA role are included.

### Infrastructure — $210

$20/month × six months = $120, plus $90 for additional services and tools. Expected result: hosting and supporting services needed for the funded work. These are spending allowances, not verified vendor quotations.

### Contingency — $240

Reserve for unforeseen project expenses within the agreed scope. Any proposed reallocation, including savings from free testing access, should be documented and agreed as applicable; the approved figures above remain unchanged.

## Unpaid maintainer contribution — $0 requested

**Product Vision, Research & Project Management — $0.**

This is the maintainer's own unpaid contribution: product strategy, research, analysis of user feedback, planning, task definition, coordination and oversight of specialists. It continues throughout the six-month period. It is additional to the funded work, excluded from the 200 budgeted specialist hours, and does not change the $10,000 request. No monetary valuation or number of unpaid hours is claimed.

## Long-term sustainability

Slowth is a long-term, free and open-source project supported through grants and voluntary donations, without mandatory subscriptions or user tracking.

This grant would fund the next six months of development. Future funding requests will be based on the project's needs and the results of this phase.
