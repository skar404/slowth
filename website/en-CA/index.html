Your apps. Your boundaries.

Choose the apps. Break the scroll.

Block any app you choose on iPhone and iPad. Or interrupt short videos and Stories across social apps with on-device content detection.

Any app you choose
Select apps in Apple’s Screen Time picker — social networks, games and anything else you want a break from. With monitoring off, selected apps are blocked; soft blocking and temporary pauses change when access is allowed.
Reels and Shorts inside the app
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, X (Twitter) and other social networks with short videos: choose the content you want to interrupt. With monitoring on, detection briefly blocks the app so you can step away.
A pause when you need one
Use a leaf for temporary access, then return to your boundaries. Apps and Safari share three pauses per day on iPhone and iPad.
It works by recognizing what’s on screen. Slowth detects short-video and Stories screens across social apps. Layouts change, so it can miss content or block an ordinary screen. Detection blocks the app temporarily; it does not remove tabs inside it.

App protection, step by step

From download to a little distance.

Choose your apps. Start monitoring when you want to detect specific content.

  1. 1

    Get Slowth

    Install Slowth, open the Apps tab and allow Screen Time access. This lets iOS block the apps you choose.

  2. 2

    Set your boundaries

    Select the apps you want to block. For supported content, choose short videos, Stories or both.

  3. 3

    Start app monitoring

    Tap Start monitoring and confirm Apple’s screen broadcast prompt. Detection runs on your device; screen images are not saved or uploaded.

Personal, by design

Your screen stays your business.

Read the privacy policy ↗

App recognition uses a screen broadcast you start yourself. While it runs, Slowth receives screen images, which can include other apps and sensitive information — choosing apps limits blocking, not screen access.

In the App Store app, images are processed temporarily in device memory. Frames are not saved or uploaded, and audio is ignored. iOS shows its recording indicator while monitoring is active.

No account, analytics or advertising SDK. Safari filtering is local; downloading rule updates still makes an ordinary network request.

Good questions

About app blocking.

More answers in Support.

Can I block Instagram Reels inside the Instagram app?

Yes. On iPhone or iPad, select Instagram, enable short-video blocking and start monitoring in Slowth. When Reels are detected, Screen Time temporarily shields the whole Instagram app. Slowth does not remove the Reels tab. You can choose Stories separately. Set up Instagram app blocking.

Can I block YouTube Shorts inside the YouTube app?

Yes. Select YouTube, enable short-video blocking and start monitoring. Slowth detects Shorts and temporarily shields YouTube. It is designed to let you use other content between detections, but recognition can make mistakes. The separate Safari extension hides Shorts on the YouTube website.

Can I block Snapchat, TikTok or another app?

Yes. You can select apps available in Apple’s Screen Time picker, including Snapchat and TikTok. With monitoring off, selected protected apps are blocked unless temporary access or soft blocking allows them. For short-video detection, turn on monitoring. Recognition depends on what is visible on screen and can make mistakes. How blocking without monitoring works.

Does it work in Facebook, X (Twitter) and other social apps?

Yes. Short-video recognition works across social apps, including Facebook and X (Twitter), rather than being limited to Instagram and YouTube. Select the app, choose short videos or Stories and start monitoring. Detection depends on the screen layout, so it may miss content or block a normal screen.

Does Safari need screen recording?

No. Safari uses its own extension to filter supported websites. Screen access is only needed for content recognition inside iPhone and iPad apps.

What happens when monitoring stops?

Selected protected apps are normally blocked without recording. Soft blocking can briefly allow an app after leaving Slowth, then block it again when used. An active temporary-access pause also allows access. Read about these modes.

Can I take a break from blocking?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, a leaf gives temporary access across selected apps and Safari. The shared pause system is experimental; iOS can delay restoring restrictions. Detection rests are separate and do not spend leaves.

What does Strict Mode lock?

For 24 hours, enabled restrictions cannot be weakened. You can add restrictions, but cannot enable whole-site blocking during the lock. The timer survives restarts. Set whether pauses are allowed before starting. It is a commitment aid, not tamper-proof parental control.

Is Slowth free? Is there an Android app?

Slowth is free and open source under GPLv3. Android is a proposed research and prototype workstream, not an available app.
