takardar kebantawa

Ƙarshe na ƙarshe: 20 Satumba 2026

Slowth baya tattara tarihin bincikenku ko abun cikin allo. Ka'idar App Store ba ta da asusu, nazari, bin diddigi, SDK talla, ko abin baya da mai haɓakawa ke sarrafa. Toshewa da binciken allo suna gudana akan na'urarka.

toshe Safari

Slowth haɓakawa ne na Safari tare da ƙa'idar aboki don iPhone, iPad, da Mac. Yana karanta shafuka masu goyan bayan gida don ɓoye ko tura abubuwan da kuka zaɓa don toshewa. Ba a aika tarihin bincike, abun cikin shafi, da zaɓin toshewa ga mai haɓakawa.

Toshewa na ainihi (Beta) - iPhone da iPad

Idan kun kunna wannan fasalin zaɓin, Apple's ReplayKit yana ba da firam ɗin allo yayin watsa shirye-shiryen yana aiki. Slowth yana amfani da Core ML akan na'urarka don gano zaɓaɓɓen Shorts, Reels, ko Labarun kuma yana tambayar iOS don nuna garkuwar Screen Time. Alamar rikodin tsarin ya kasance bayyane yayin da saka idanu ke aiki.

Ba a adana ko loda firam ɗin allo yayin amfani na yau da kullun na aikace-aikacen App Store. Ba a sarrafa samfuran sauti ba. Kuna iya dakatar da saka idanu ta amfani da sarrafa rikodi. Abubuwan kamawa masu haɓakawa da lodawa an iyakance su ne don gina ginin gyara kuma babu su a cikin app ɗin App Store.

A kan Mac, Slowth yana toshe abun ciki kawai a cikin Safari; baya lura da allon ko toshe abun ciki a cikin wasu apps.

Abin da ke kan na'urar ku

Saituna, kowane rukunin yanar gizo, madaidaicin lokaci mai ƙima, alamun zaɓin app, da matsayin aiki na gida ana adana su akan na'urarka. Dokokin toshewa da aka zazzage ana adana su a cikin gida. Ba a aika waɗannan saituna da dokoki zuwa ga mai haɓakawa.

Sabunta doka da buƙatun hanyar sadarwa

Tsawaita Safari lokaci-lokaci yana sauke fayil ɗin toshewar jama'a daga GitHub Gist akan HTTPS. Dokokin iri ɗaya suna samuwa ga kowa. Buƙatun baya haɗa da tarihin bincike, abun cikin shafi, firam ɗin allo, ko zaɓin app ɗin ku.

GitHub yana karɓar bayanan haɗin kai na yau da kullun, kamar adireshin IP ɗin ku, lokacin yin hidimar fayil ɗin. Manufar sirrinta tana aiki.

Screen Time da Family Controls — iOS kawai

Tare da izinin ku, Slowth yana amfani da tsarin Family Controls na Apple da Managed Settings don toshe ƙa'idodin da aka zaɓa. Zaɓuɓɓukan ku ana wakilta ta alamun kariya ta sirri, an adana su cikin gida kuma ana amfani da su don tambayar iOS don aiwatar da toshe app.

Yanar Gizo da sabis na waje

Ana amfani da gidan yanar gizon Slowth ta Shafukan GitHub da Cloudflare. Ziyartar ta yana haifar da buƙatun yanar gizo na yau da kullun, don haka waɗannan masu samarwa suna karɓar bayanan haɗin kai kamar adireshin IP ɗin ku. Bude wasu hanyoyin haɗin waje ana sarrafa su ta hanyar manufofin keɓantawar wurin. Hanyoyin haɗin harshe a wannan shafin suna aiki ba tare da kukis ko JavaScript ba.
