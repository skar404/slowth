Set up app protection, step by step

Set up app blocking in three steps.

Choose your apps. Start monitoring when you wan detect particular content.

  1. 1

    Get Slowth

    Install Slowth, open the Apps tab and allow Screen Time access. This go let iOS block the apps wey you choose.

  2. 2

    Choose your blocking rules

    Select the apps wey you wan block. For content wey detection support, choose short videos, Stories or both.

  3. 3

    Start app monitoring

    Tap Start monitoring and confirm Apple screen broadcast prompt. Detection dey run for your device; screen images no dey save or upload.

We build am with privacy in mind

Wetin dey your screen go remain private.

Read the privacy policy ↗

App recognition dey use screen broadcast wey you start yourself. As e dey run, Slowth dey receive screen images wey fit include other apps and sensitive information. The apps wey you choose na the ones wey blocking go affect; your choice no limit wetin screen access fit see.

For the App Store app, screen images dey process temporarily inside your device memory. Frames no dey save or upload, and audio no dey process. iOS go show recording indicator while monitoring dey active.

No account, analytics or advertising SDK. Safari filtering dey happen for your device; downloading rule updates still dey make ordinary network request.

Questions wey people dey ask

About app blocking.

You go find more answers for Help.

I fit block Instagram Reels inside the Instagram app?

Yes. For iPhone or iPad, select Instagram, enable short-video blocking and start monitoring inside Slowth. When e detect Reels, Screen Time go block the whole Instagram app for small time. Slowth no dey remove the Reels tab. You fit choose Stories separately. Set up Instagram app blocking.

I fit block YouTube Shorts inside the YouTube app?

Yes. Select YouTube, enable short-video blocking and start monitoring. Slowth go detect Shorts and block YouTube for small time. E dey designed to let you use other content between detections, but recognition fit make mistake. The separate Safari extension dey hide Shorts for YouTube website.

I fit block Snapchat, TikTok or another app?

Yes. You fit select apps wey dey Apple Screen Time picker, including Snapchat and TikTok. When monitoring dey off, the protected apps wey you select go dey blocked unless temporary access or soft blocking allow them. Start monitoring to detect short videos. Recognition depend on wetin dey visible for the screen, and e fit make mistake. How blocking dey work without monitoring.

E dey work for Facebook, X (Twitter) and other social apps?

Yes. Short-video recognition dey work across social apps, including Facebook and X (Twitter); e no end for Instagram and YouTube. Select the app, choose short videos or Stories and start monitoring. Detection depend on how the screen arrange, so e fit miss content or block normal screen.

Safari need screen recording?

No. Safari dey use im own extension to filter supported websites. Screen access na only for content recognition inside iPhone and iPad apps.

Wetin go happen when monitoring stop?

Normally, the protected apps wey you select go dey blocked when recording no dey run. Soft blocking fit allow app for small time after you leave Slowth, then block am again when you use am. Temporary-access pause wey still dey active fit allow access too. Read about these modes.

I fit take break from blocking?

Yes. For iPhone and iPad, one leaf go give temporary access across selected apps and Safari. The shared pause system still dey experimental; iOS fit delay before e restore restrictions. Rest after detection na different thing, and e no dey use leaves.

Wetin Strict Mode dey lock?

For 24 hours, you no fit weaken restrictions wey you don enable. You fit add restrictions, but you no fit enable whole-site blocking while the lock dey active. The timer no go reset if you restart. Choose whether pauses dey allowed before you start. Na tool to help you stick to your plan; e no be parental control wey person no fit bypass.

Slowth free? Android app dey?

Slowth free and the source code dey open under GPLv3. Android na proposed research and prototype work; no Android app dey available yet.
