Apps · Only for iPhone & iPad

Block Reels and Shorts inside apps.

To block Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or short videos for Facebook, X (Twitter) and other social apps, select the app and follow the steps below. You fit also select apps like Snapchat, TikTok and games to block the whole app. See how blocking dey work without monitoring.

  1. Open the Apps tab and allow Screen Time access when the prompt show. This go let Slowth ask iOS to block the selected apps. E no go start recording.
  2. Choose the apps wey you wan block for Apple Screen Time picker. If you choose category, e go select the apps wey dey inside am now; na only apps e go save, website selections no count. Select the category again after you install more apps.
  3. Tap an app to choose im rules for short videos, Stories and soft blocking. For new selections, both content types go dey blocked and soft blocking go dey off.
  4. Tap Start monitoring for the Apps tab, select Slowth for Apple broadcast prompt if e ask, and confirm the start. Wait make monitoring become active before you go back to an app.
  5. When Slowth detect selected content, Screen Time go block the whole app. Wait for the short rest countdown, then leave that content when you return.
Recognition depend on wetin dey the screen. Slowth dey recognize short-video and Stories screens across social apps, including Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and X (Twitter). App layouts fit change, and recognition fit miss content or block ordinary screens by mistake. Selecting an app alone no go start monitoring.

Understand screen access

The broadcast dey supply images of your current screen. E fit include other apps and sensitive information. The apps wey you select na the ones wey blocking go affect; your selection no limit wetin dey inside the broadcast. For the App Store app, frames dey process temporarily for your device; dem no dey save or upload. Audio no dey process.

Stop monitoring

Go back to Slowth, tap Stop monitoring and confirm the system prompt. You fit also use the iOS recording indicator to stop the broadcast. Check say the indicator don stop. When you stop monitoring, app protection go change as we explain below; the Safari extension no go turn off.

Safari · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Start with your browser.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Install and open Slowth.
  2. For iOS Settings, open Apps → Safari → Extensions → Slowth and enable the extension. For older iOS versions, Safari dey directly inside Settings.
  3. Allow access to the supported websites wey you wan make Slowth filter. Use All Websites → Allow if you wan cover all supported sites.
  4. Open Slowth Safari settings and choose the content wey you wan block for each site. Reload pages wey don already open for Safari.

Mac

  1. Open Safari → Settings → Extensions and enable Slowth.
  2. Allow website access when Safari ask.
  3. Choose settings for each site inside Slowth and reload the page.

Safari no need Screen Time permission or screen broadcast. YouTube get Shorts controls; Instagram and Facebook get Reels and feed controls; X cover Explore and trends. You fit also block supported websites completely. TikTok get whole-site blocking.

Use Update rules now to check whether the public blocking rules don change. Rule updates need network connection, and you no fit apply them while Strict Mode dey active.

When recording dey off.

Normally, the protected apps wey you select go dey blocked when broadcast no dey run. If you stop recording, that no mean say you go get unrestricted access to those apps.

Soft blocking
If soft blocking dey enabled for an app, Slowth go allow am for small time after you leave Slowth, then ask iOS to block am when you use am. This fit help background audio. Picture in Picture fit still dey blocked; no be unrestricted mode.
Temporary access
Allow content go open the whole selected group for the pause duration. E dey work even when recording no dey run. When access end, blocking go resume, although iOS fit delay before e apply am.

Start monitoring again to use content recognition. If you no fit change a setting, check whether Strict Mode dey active.

Two different kinds of pause.

Rest after detection

After Slowth detect content wey you choose to block, a short countdown go give you small break from the app. These automatic rests dey last 5–15 seconds and no dey use leaves. You fit close Slowth while the countdown continue. Leave the blocked content when you return, or another rest fit start.

Use a leaf for temporary access

For iPhone and iPad, apps and Safari share three pauses each day. Allow content go use one leaf to allow blocked content across the selected group for small time. For Settings → Pause duration, choose 30 seconds, 1 minute or 3 minutes for new pauses. The timer dey show how much access remain; Block again go end temporary access early and restore blocking. E no go return the leaf wey you spend.

Leaves wey you no use go enter your saved reward count after the day end; saved leaves no be extra access. Shared pauses still dey experimental, and iOS fit delay before e restore restrictions. You no need screen broadcast for temporary access.

Strict Mode and im limits.

Strict Mode dey lock restrictions wey you enable for 24 hours. If you close the app or restart the device, the timer go continue. You no fit end am early inside Slowth.

  • You no fit weaken restrictions wey you don enable. You fit add restrictions, but you no fit enable whole-site blocking while the lock dey active.
  • You no fit refresh rules while Strict Mode dey active.
  • For iPhone and iPad, decide whether to Allow pauses in Strict mode before you start. If you disallow pauses, you no fit enable them again while the lock dey active.

Strict Mode na tool to help you stick to your own plan. E no make the device impossible to bypass, and e no stop every change outside Slowth, like removing the app or changing system permissions. Check your settings before you enable am.

Something no dey work well?

Shorts or Reels still dey show for Safari

Check say the extension dey enabled, e get permission for that website, and the blocking option wey you need dey on. Reload the page. If Strict Mode no dey active, update rules and try again. Website layouts dey change; if e still fail, send the site name, OS version and the steps wey go make the problem happen again.

App dey blocked as soon as I open am

Check monitoring status inside Slowth. If broadcast no dey active, protected apps wey you select normally go dey blocked. Start monitoring for content detection, or use temporary-access pause if one dey available. Also check whether rest after detection still dey active.

Monitoring dey stop, or e no dey start

Go back to Slowth and start monitoring again through Apple prompt. Check the system recording indicator and Screen Time permission. iOS fit interrupt screen broadcasts; Slowth no fit secretly start one again for you. If e dey stop repeatedly, report am with your device and OS version.

Slowth block the wrong screen or miss content

Recognition no dey perfect. Check the content rules for the selected app and whether detection support the content. You fit report wetin happen through feedback inside the app or by email. No send sensitive screen content; describe the app, screen and steps instead.

Pause don end, but access no change immediately

The pause timer and the system blocking screen na separate things: iOS fit delay before e restore restrictions. Open Slowth again to check im current status. When you report the issue, include the pause duration and whether monitoring dey run.

Setting dey locked or app no dey show

Check the Strict Mode timer and review the apps wey you select for the system picker. Na only apps e dey save; websites no count. If you select a category before installing an app, select that category again.

You still need help?

Tell me wetin happen.

Send email to denis@malina.page or open an issue for GitHub. Include Slowth version, device and OS version, the steps wey cause the problem, and wetin you expect. Tell us whether e happen for Safari or inside an app, and whether monitoring, a pause or Strict Mode dey active.
