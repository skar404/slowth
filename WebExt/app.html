Guide
What Strict mode does
For 24 hours, enabled restrictions cannot be turned off. You can add restrictions, but cannot enable whole-site blocking. The timer survives restarts and force-quits.
Turn it on when willpower alone isn't enough. Leave it off if you just want a soft nudge.
How blocking works
Off The site loads as usual. Slowth does nothing.
Site-specific block Blocks YouTube Shorts, Instagram/Facebook Reels, or X Explore and trends. The rest of the site keeps working.
Block Infinite Feed Infinite Feed limits scrolling. On Instagram it also includes Explore and Stories.
Block site Visiting the site shows a friendly blocked page instead.
